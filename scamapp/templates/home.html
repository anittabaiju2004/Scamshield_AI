Understand Your Security Risk

Every submission is classified into one of three clear risk levels.

Low Risk check_circle

Content looks normal: trusted sender, established domain and a valid SSL certificate.

Safe to interact. Standard precautions apply.
Medium Risk warning

Suspicious signs such as unexplained urgency, a newly created domain or odd wording.

Don't click unknown links. Verify the sender another way.
High Risk dangerous

Matches known threats: fake login pages, malware links or blacklisted domains.

Block the sender. Do not share any information.

Note: Risk ratings are automated assessments and are not a guarantee of safety. Always stay cautious.
